Permanent Bonuses & Missables

  • Auntie Ethel's Hair → +1 CHA Act 1, before fighting her. Let her take one eye, then REFUSE — she gives the hair. Do not kill her first.
  • Necromancy of Thay → Speak with Dead Act 1, Blighted Village basement bookshelf. Requires Tome of Thay key from phase spider area.
  • Loviatar's Love → +2 attacks/saves below 30% HP Act 1, let Abdirak beat you in goblin camp dungeon until you receive the buff.
  • Find Familiar: Scratch Act 1, pet the dog near Blighted Village, find his owner's body, keep him.
  • Survival Instinct Act 3.
  • Mirror of Loss → +2 CHA Act 3, Gauntlet under House of Grief. Use Guidance + save scum. Do this first in the Mirror order.

Combat Tactics

  • Turn 1: Hex (bonus action) + Eldritch Blast (action) for early reliable damage
  • Mid-game: Hypnotic Pattern or Hunger of Hadar for crowd control; Cutting Words to protect allies
  • Act 3: Greater Invisibility on Astarion before his nova turn; Animate Dead for summons; Slow to cripple enemy action economy
  • Hex competes with concentration spells — drop it once you have Hypnotic Pattern / Hunger of Hadar / Greater Invisibility available

Per-Turn Damage Breakdown — Act 3 Full Build

  • Action: 2 attacks (Extra Attack)
  • Bonus Action 1: Flurry of Blows: Topple (2 punches, CON save or prone)
  • Bonus Action 2: Ki Resonation Punch OR second Flurry (Fast Hands)
  • Each punch: 1d8 (Martial Arts) + 16 (Tavern Brawler × Cloud Giant mod) + WIS mod (Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo) + 1d6 Sneak Attack + 1d4+WIS (Manifestation of Mind) + 1d10 Force + 10 HP heal (Gloves of Soul Catching)
  • Total: 4–6 hits per turn · ~25–35 damage each · 100–200 damage per turn consistently

Combat Tactics

  • Open with Stunning Strike on the most dangerous enemy (boss/caster) — stunned = auto-crit, all attacks have advantage. Spend Ki freely; Sentient Amulet restores Ki mid-combat.
  • Flurry of Blows: Topple on clusters — prone enemies give Astarion advantage on all ranged attacks, enabling Sneak Attack and Sharpshooter reliably
  • Ki Resonation: Blast for AoE detonation on clustered enemies
  • Patient Defense when low on Ki or taking heavy fire
  • Sentient Amulet: Ki Restoration mid-combat — more Stunning Strikes, more prone, more advantage for the party

Party Synergies

SynergyCharactersHow It Works
Prone → Advantage Karlach + Astarion Karlach Flurry: Topple knocks prone; Astarion gets advantage on all ranged attacks vs prone targets, enabling Sneak Attack and Sharpshooter reliably every turn
Stun → Auto-crit Karlach + Everyone Karlach Stunning Strike stuns the target; all melee attacks auto-crit. Astarion's Action Surge turn against a stunned boss is catastrophic.
Radiant Synergy Shadowheart Luminous Armour marks enemies that hit her as Luminous; Callous Glow Ring adds 2 radiant damage to all attacks vs Luminous; Coruscation Ring makes hit enemies glow too — creates a chain

Slow Malevola + All Slow halves enemy action economy; stacks with Stunning Strike for fully action-locked bosses
Heroes' Feast Shadowheart + All Cast before every major fight — immunity to fear/poison, +15 max HP, advantage on WIS saves for entire party
